Our Story

At STEAKZ, we believe that the perfect steak is a dialogue between ancient wood-fired techniques and modern culinary precision. Our heritage is rooted in the heat of the British hearth and the patience of the aging room.

Every cut is hand-selected from heritage British breeds, aged for a minimum of 45 days, and finished over high-intensity white oak flames. This is not just dining; it is a meticulous study in flavour and temperature.
